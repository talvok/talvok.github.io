  • Portfolio business: Impostazioni business → App → rimuovi «Talvok»

Alla revoca i token di accesso smettono immediatamente di funzionare e vengono eliminati dal computer dello studio.

Data Deletion

The Talvok application publishes only to the project's own social accounts and stores no personal data belonging to third parties, so there is no third-party data to delete. The account owner can revoke the application's access at any time from each platform's "Apps and websites" settings; access tokens stop working immediately and are deleted locally.
